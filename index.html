Han Wang, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor
Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
The University of Kansas

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Kansas, where I joined in Fall 2023. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Illinois Institute of Technology, advised by Yuan Hong, and my M.S. in Software Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on data privacy and security and trustworthy machine learning, including differential privacy, privacy-preserving collaborative learning, adversarial robustness, membership inference, and machine unlearning.

Research Interests

  • Data Privacy and Security

    Differential privacy, local differential privacy, private data analysis, secure multiparty computation, and applied cryptography.

  • Privacy-Preserving Collaborative Learning

    Federated learning, split learning, and defenses against inference and gradient inversion attacks.

  • Trustworthy Machine Learning

    Adversarial robustness, membership inference, machine unlearning, and privacy auditing.
